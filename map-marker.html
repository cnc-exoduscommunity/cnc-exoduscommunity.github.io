Mark spots on the map images. Click the map to add a mark of the chosen kind, drag a mark to move it, click a mark to edit it (name, note, pictures). Your work is kept in this browser until you're done; then copy the text at the bottom into _data/map_marks.yml.

Tip: scroll or pinch to zoom, drag the map to move it (a click adds a mark). Arrow keys nudge the selected mark (hold Shift for bigger steps), Delete removes it, Esc deselects, + / - / 0 zoom.
